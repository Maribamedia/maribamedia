Disclaimer & Compliance

Mariba Media (Reg: 2024/132788/07) connects South African businesses to a network of specialist partners. Mariba Media does not hold, process or transact any funds. It does not provide financial advice, intermediary services or any other regulated financial service as defined in the Financial Advisory and Intermediary Services Act (FAIS Act, 37 of 2002). All financial services are rendered solely by the regulated partners within the network.

Mariba Media's function is to accept client project information and connect that information unchanged to the relevant partner with the exclusive mandate. No curation, feasibility review, recommendation or filtering of any kind is performed by Mariba Media. All funding terms, credit decisions and due diligence are the exclusive responsibility of the partner that engages with the client. No funding outcome is guaranteed.

Solar, IPP funding and business finance services are delivered by our specialist partners, each of which is a licensed credit provider or authorised financial services provider. Mariba Media does not participate in any installation, maintenance or credit decision.
